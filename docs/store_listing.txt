App name
ASS SAUVEGARDE DES SITES DE LA

Short description
Book appointments, track tasks, payroll reports, and assets offline.

Full description
ASS SAUVEGARDE DES SITES DE LA helps operations teams organize everyday business work from one Android phone. The app keeps appointment booking, task follow-up, payroll report notes, and asset tracking in a focused mobile workspace.

Teams can add local records, review priorities, advance status, and open detail views without creating an account or connecting to a server. It is built for field supervisors, office coordinators, and site managers who need clear daily control.
